# CF2 proposal: CF trained twice as long, with a matching D1 control (2026-11-25)

Status (2026-11-25): A launched 10:30 EST (jobs 15095–15100) and B launched 10:35 EST (jobs 15177–15182), both on the user's go-ahead; see "A and B as launched" at the end. D1 and CF are frozen
(runs/fallback_d1_reference_2026-11-24, runs/frozen_cf_2026-11-25; read-only with SHA256SUMS).

## Where CF stands (audit §63)

- **Keeps:** frame invariance (24 relabellings, spread ≤ 0.002), and clear gains on hands with a different frame
  convention. On Wuji, contact error −0.86 mm (3/3); on Shadow, −1.53 mm (3/3) and wrist −0.80 mm (3/3). CF is the only model
  whose Shadow wrist beats no movement.
- **Costs:** held-out LEAP contact error +0.17 mm (significant in seed 0 only, which failed Primary 1). Wrist rotation is
  +0.14–0.18° worse on Allegro / LEAP / held-out LEAP (significant in 2/3 seeds each). Source-val generated bodies +0.12 mm and
  held-out generated bodies +0.26 mm (training-log validation, 3-seed means).

## Diagnosis (no training; source validation and training logs, plus the held-out LEAP split)

1. **Not the frame rule's per-variant error.** Every LEAP variant's canonical frame is within 11° of the LEAP mean frame
   (held-out: ≤ 7°). Per-variant CF − D1 contact error does not track that angle (correlation +0.10 on 12 source-val
   variants, −0.09 on 4 held-out variants). The one 87° outlier (I0-M4-R0-T3_g0) is a training variant and is not in either
   evaluation split.
2. **The costs fall on the families whose native frame was already one fixed convention** (LEAP variants, generated bodies).
   They are absent on single hands (Allegro, Barrett) and reversed on hands with a different convention (Wuji, Shadow).
   Reading: D1 could use each family's fixed frame convention as a shortcut for telling families apart and specialising.
   CF removes that shortcut, so it has to learn the same distinctions from geometry alone. That is a harder fit, not a frame
   error.
3. **Both models were still improving when the schedule ended.** Source-val LEAP contact error fell 40k → 50k by 0.08–0.10 mm
   (D1) and 0.09–0.11 mm (CF) while the cosine learning rate went to zero. Best checkpoints were at 45–50k steps. CF's
   held-out LEAP gap to D1 stays roughly constant through training (+0.2 to +0.3 mm from 20k to 50k), so this is evidence
   of under-training in general, not proof that the gap closes.

Hypothesis: CF needs more optimisation to recover the in-distribution specialisation D1 got from the frame shortcut. A longer
schedule keeps every CF property by construction (same frame, data, losses and model), so the gains on unseen hands
should not be at risk.

## Proposal

- **CF2** = CF with one change: `total_steps` 50,000 → 100,000 (the cosine schedule stretches; warm-up unchanged). Seeds
  0/1/2. Configs `cf2_long_hf{,_s1,_s2}`.
- **D1-long control** = D1 with the same one change. Configs `d1_long_hf{,_s1,_s2}`. Needed because D1 was also still
  improving. Without it, a CF2 gain over D1 could just be training length. This is an extra arm, listed here for approval.
- Generated by `scripts/decoder/dlevels/make_cf2.py`. The diff check confirms only total_steps / out_dir / _note change.
- **Cost:** 6 runs. CF2 is about 3.6 h each at the measured 460 steps/min (D1-long about 3 h). They run in parallel if GPUs
  allow, with a 5 h time limit to avoid a requeue mid-run. The partition, constraint and exclude list are unchanged
  (default_partition only).
- **Benchmark:** `configs/decoder/benchmark/transfer_v1_cf2.json`, comparing D1 / CF (50k) and D1-long / CF2 (100k) paired by
  seed, with Shadow included. Plus the relabelling test on CF2 (expected spread ≈ 0).

## Predeclared readout

- **Primary 1 (non-inferiority, as for CF):** CF2 vs D1-long on source Allegro / Barrett / LEAP and held-out LEAP contact error
  and contact success. No significant loss in any seed.
- **Primary 2 (keeps CF's gain):** CF2 vs D1-long on Shadow wrist mm significantly better and translation R²_id higher, 3/3.
- **Primary 3 (added 2026-11-25 at the user's request, before any A or B result existed): better than D1 on contact error
  across all families.** For each candidate (CF-long, and CF-bv for B), contact error (corr_mm) must be lower than the D1
  comparator's in every seed on each family:
  - benchmark pools (paired within seed): source Allegro, Barrett, LEAP; held-out LEAP; Wuji; Shadow;
  - training-log validation at the selected checkpoint (no paired CI is available there, so the point estimate decides):
    source-val generated bodies and held-out generated bodies (P0800–P0849).
  - Comparators: the matched D1 (D1-long for CF-long, D1-bv for CF-bv), and the frozen delivery D1 (50k).
  - Per family, report "lower in k/3 seeds" and "significantly lower in k/3". The criterion holds for a family only if
    lower in 3/3 seeds and never significantly higher.
  - At 50k, CF was lower than D1 on Wuji and Shadow in 3/3, but not on the training families (Allegro 2/3, Barrett 2/3,
    LEAP 1/3, held-out LEAP 0/3), so this is a strict bar.
- **Delivery with Primary 3:** a CF model becomes the delivery default only if Primaries 1–3 all hold against its matched D1.
  If Primaries 1–2 hold but not 3, report which families block it, and the choice goes back to the user.
- **Secondary:** CF2 vs CF (does length help CF?), D1-long vs D1, Wuji / Shadow contacts, wrist rotation on source.
- **Delivery:** if both primaries hold, CF2 replaces D1 as the delivery default. If only Primary 2 holds, D1-long (or D1,
  whichever source validation favours) stays the default and CF2 is the unseen-hand alternative. 3/3 counts; no seed is
  chosen; nothing is selected on Wuji or Shadow.
- **Caveat:** held-out LEAP informed this diagnosis, so for CF2 it is no longer an untouched split. The held-out generated
  bodies (P0800–P0849, training-log validation) are reported alongside as a split this design did not look at per body.

## Not proposed (one change at a time)

- A larger model.
- LEAP up-weighting: it would change the data balance and could cost the unseen-hand gains.
- Small frame jitter around the canonical frame: diagnosis 1 does not point to frame error.
- Further changes to the frame rule.

Revisit only after CF2.

## A and B as launched (2026-11-25, user go-ahead)

The user asked to scale both models before comparing them, so the D1-long control is part of the comparison (A). B was added
to test the diagnosis directly.
- **A (scale):** d1_long_hf* and cf2_long_hf* (CF-long), 100k steps, 3 seeds each. Auto-evaluation slurm/cf2_chain.sh →
  transfer_v1_cf2.json (D1, CF, D1-long, CF-long) + relabelling test.
- **B (body variety):** d1_bv_long_hf* and cf_bv_long_hf*. A plus one change: LEAP 414 hands (all 138 train-split topologies
  × 3 geometry draws) × 48 records, and generated bodies P0000–P0799 × 25 records. The record budget and family shares are
  the same as A, and val / extra_eval are identical. Made by scripts/decoder/dlevels/make_bodyvar.py, manifest
  configs/decoder/dlevels/bodyvar_manifest.json.
  - Validation: the 366 LEAP hands new in B all pass the G1 checks (scripts/decoder/dlevels/validate_leap_bv.py,
    configs/decoder/dlevels/bodyvar_validation_*of8.json).
  - GPU smoke (200 steps, 1,270 robots canonicalised): checks pass.
  - Auto-evaluation slurm/bv_chain.sh → transfer_v1_bv.json (D1-long, CF-long, D1-bv, CF-bv) + relabelling test on CF-bv.
- **B readout:** CF-bv vs CF-long on held-out LEAP and held-out generated-body contact error (does variety close CF's gap on
  new bodies of known families?), and CF-bv vs D1-bv with the same two primaries as A. Same rules: 3/3 seeds, no seed chosen,
  nothing selected on Wuji or Shadow.
- **Primary 3 scoring:** scripts/decoder/benchmark/cf2_summary.py (--cand / --ref; generated-body families from the training
  log at the selected step). Dry run on the 50k results (CF vs D1) reproduces the counts above. transfer_v1_bv.json also
  includes the frozen D1 (50k) for the second comparator.

## A results (2026-11-25, evaluation complete 13:34 EST)

Readouts: configs/decoder/dlevels/cf2_readout/a_{cf2_vs_d1long,cf2_vs_d1,d1long_vs_d1,cf2_vs_cf}.txt (cf2_summary.py on
transfer_benchmark_v1_cf2/metrics.json). Best steps: D1-long 90k / 97.5k / 97.5k; CF-long 87.5k / 95k / 100k.
Relabelling test on CF-long: max metric spread over 24 relabellings 0, world error 6e-8 m.

- **CF-long vs D1-long (matched):** Primary 1 HOLDS (no significant loss in any seed). Primary 2 HOLDS (Shadow wrist
  −0.94 mm, significant 3/3; trans R²_id higher 3/3). Primary 3 FAILS: lower on Allegro, Barrett, LEAP, Wuji, Shadow (3/3),
  but held-out LEAP +0.12 mm (higher in 3/3, none significant), source-val generated bodies higher 2/3 (+0.14 mean) and
  held-out generated bodies higher 2/3 (+0.28 mean).
- **CF-long vs frozen D1 (50k):** Primaries 1 and 2 HOLD. Primary 3 fails only on held-out LEAP: lower in 2/3 (−0.37, −0.25,
  significant), seed 0 +0.014 (not significant). Every other family lower 3/3, including both generated-body families.
- **Length helps both:** D1-long vs D1 lowers source and held-out LEAP contact error (held-out −0.32 mm), but Shadow gets worse
  (corr +0.78, significant in 1/3; wrist worse in 1/3). CF-long vs CF: lower contact error on all four training-family
  pools and both generated-body sets in 3/3 (held-out LEAP −0.37 mm); Wuji / Shadow mixed (better in seed 0, worse in 1–2).
- **Delivery under the predeclared rule:** not met (Primary 3 fails against the matched D1). Blocking families: held-out LEAP
  and generated bodies (held-out and source-val). Choice goes back to the user; B (body variety) targets exactly these.

## B results (2026-11-25, evaluation complete 14:23 EST)

Readouts: configs/decoder/dlevels/cf2_readout/b_*.txt (transfer_benchmark_v1_bv/metrics.json). Best steps: D1-bv 92.5k / 97.5k /
87.5k; CF-bv 87.5k / 97.5k / 100k. Relabelling test on CF-bv: max spread 0.

- **CF-bv vs D1-bv (matched):** Primary 1 FAILS (seed 0 significantly worse on source Allegro / Barrett / LEAP corr and
  held-out LEAP). Primary 2 FAILS (Shadow wrist significant in 2/3). Primary 3 FAILS on every family:
  generated bodies +0.39 / +0.55 mm (3/3 higher), held-out LEAP +0.17, Wuji +0.32.
- **Body variety helped D1, not CF.** D1-bv vs frozen D1: contact error lower in 3/3 on Allegro, Barrett, LEAP, held-out LEAP
  (−0.39 mm, all significant), Wuji (−0.79, all significant) and both generated sets (held-out −0.84 mean); Shadow lower 2/3
  (−0.63 mean; seed 1 +0.15, not significant). CF-bv vs CF-long: no gain on held-out LEAP (−0.02), worse on source
  generated bodies (+0.29), Wuji and Shadow worse in 2/3. The diagnosis (CF's gap is a lack of body variety) is not supported.
- **D1-bv vs CF-long (the two strongest):** tie on source (no consistent sign). D1-bv better on held-out LEAP (−0.19 mm, 3/3
  lower) and held-out generated bodies (−0.64). CF-long better on Shadow (contact −0.61 mm, wrist −0.50 mm significant 3/3,
  trans R²_id 0.03–0.09 vs −0.11–0.01). Wuji: tie (−0.04).
- **Transfer beyond no movement:** both beat no movement on every pool and metric in 3/3, except Shadow wrist: CF-long better
  in 2/3 (−0.35 mm), D1-bv not (+0.14 mm mean, worse in 1/3).
- **Delivery:** no CF model meets Primaries 1–3 against its matched D1, so per the rule above the choice goes to the user.
