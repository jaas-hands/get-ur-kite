**1. Finish what is running, as designed.**
- CF-3g (`docs/cf3g_split_teachers.md`): 9 fold teachers -> go/no-go (checks 1 and 2, generated bodies) -> on GO, 3 `cf3g_split`

- Teacher hand-off to AWS (user's rule): if preemptions continue and any teacher is projected to finish after 11:45 EST
  (11 h after launch). Aryan's data checks and smoke run pass (his step-100 loss equals ours); a moved run is stopped here first.

**2. Then stop teacher / size / length tweaks.** After CF-3g, long, bigext and big300k gate, no more arms of that kind.
*Open:* Aryan suggested pausing big300k early if long and bigext show nothing (his compute).

**3. Fix how we measure, before the next gate is written.**
- Every gate also reports corr_success, compat_success, the median and the 90th percentile per hand, not only mean corr_mm.
- Tolerances anchored to measured seed spread (table above), not a fixed 0.10 mm.
- Interim snapshots and training-log readings are descriptive, never near-verdicts.
- Each gate frozen in a commit before launch.
- Any winner confirmed with 3 fresh seeds before it replaces CF-3e, then on Simon's splits.
- Wuji, Shadow and the 7 external hands are new-family validation; *open:* a sealed fresh new-family test set (3-5 public hands
  never evaluated, scored once at the end).

**4. Next research directions (after the running gates), in this order.**
1. Frame robustness. Rule-E frame flips and origin conventions are the largest known error on new families (9 flip hands:
   CF-3e 10.0-10.1 vs D1-long 8.1-8.4 mm; MetaHand 10.3-11.6 vs D1 7.4-8.3). Per-hand frame selection at inference (32 sample
   requests per new hand) already fixed most of MetaHand and half the flip gap; it is close to ready.
2. Error breakdown. A true-contact bound for CF-3e's own decoder (evaluation only, about 1-2 GPU-h + a small script) splits the
   remaining error between contact prediction and the decoder (the older decoder: true contacts 5.71/5.60/7.90/8.28 vs predicted
   6.31/6.31/8.30/8.85). *Open:* CF-3e without distillation, same recipe (3 seeds x 4-6 h), to size what distillation buys.
3. Multi-step refinement. The decoder takes one step from a 14-15 mm start to about 7-8.5 mm; earlier, iterating on its own
   output made things worse. Training it to refine step by step is a new line and needs a design first.
